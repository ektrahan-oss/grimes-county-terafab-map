Public Comments 
We look forward to hear from the citizens of Grimes County. If you wish to make public comments you will need to 
submit a Public Participation Form prior to the start of the meeting and give it to the County Clerk. Public 
comments will be limited to 2 minutes per person. 
 
Speaker Ron Boffa: Exclusion of paving CR 206 North. Mr Boffa stated he is before the Court again to discuss the 
decision to exclude the northern 6/10 of a mile of 206 from the current paving project on CR 206 South and CR 
313. 206 N is the most densely populated  portion  with 13 properties. The less populated CR 206 S is included in 
the project. Mr Boffa spoke with County Engineer Jon Steiber he learned that the road budget had been reduced 
from $2.5 million to $2 million. The cost of paving 206 N was estimated at $60,000, 3% of the total budget. Mr 
Boffa stated that paving this short section now while crews, equipment and materials on site would save the county 
money by eliminating the need for repeated maintenance. 2 final points made were residents have been asking for 
paving improvements for 15 years and have been told some day or maybe 2027 but without commitment. Second 
the community appeared before Court expressing concerns about health impacts, dust, traffic volume of 40+ 
vehicles per day, property values, tax fairness and overall quality of life. Their community is asking firmly but 
respectfully for action. 
Pct 3 Commissioner Michael Raska: Reminding the County there is a clean up next weekend to take trash to 
dumpsites. County Judge Joe Fauth asked if there is a listing of what the dumpsites cannot accept. Asked Dione 
Bumpous to make sure that is listed on the website. 
Pct 4 Commissioner Phillip Cox: Asking the County Engineer Jon Steiber if he will be addressing the tire removal? 
If not to make sure the county is aware of a significant amount of tires that were dumped on one of the county roads 
in Commissioner Cox precinct. Jon Steiber along with his road crew, along with the Sheriffs Dept and the inmate 
trustee program did an outstanding job of removing the tires. At last count there was 30,000 lbs of tires. Those are 
the things that impact budgets that prevent other projects from being completed. There are other instances 
throughout the county and the Road Dept does what they can to prevent  EPA ramifications and fines that can be 
levied against the county. If anyone is aware of any illegal dumping, please notify the Sheriffs Dept, Constable's 
office or Commissioners.  The County Judge stated that we thought there was a resource to take the tires but they 
were too badly decomposed to be reused.
