            3. Discuss and take action to request additional funds from the county for the Jail
               Inmate Medical Line Item. — Martha Smith, Grimes  County  Sheriff's Office
               Business Manager GSO   Michelle Weeks reported that the sheriff's office requires an
               additional fifteen thousand dollars to cover jail inmate medical expenses through the end
               of the month, as several bills came in larger than anticipated. County Judge Joe Fauth
               asked County Auditor Jessi Murphy to identify available funds, and Murphy confirmed
               that the court still had remaining funds in the contingency line sufficient to cover the
               requested amount. After asking the commissioners whether they anticipated any other

               FY 2026 contingency  budget to the Sheriff's Office jail inmate medical line item.

            4. Discuss and take action to approve a proclamation proclaiming  National Night Out
               in Grimes County, Texas, and recognizing  its importance in promoting community
               partnerships and public safety. — Martha Smith, Grimes  County Sheriff's Office
               County Judge Joe Fauth read a proclamation designating the first Tuesday in October of
               each year as National Night Out in Grimes County, Texas. The proclamation recognized
               National Night Out as a community building campaign that promotes police community
               partnerships and neighborhood camaraderie to enhance public safety and quality of life.
               The document noted that the annual event is designed to heighten crime and drug
               prevention awareness, generate support for local anticrime efforts, strengthen
               neighborhood spirit, and send a message to criminals that organized neighborhoods are
               fighting back. Judge Fauth emphasized that recognizing this effort annually helps
               maintain and grow the partnership between the community and law enforcement.
               Following the reading, Business Manager GSO Michelle Weeks was thanked for her
               work on the matter, and attendees were informed that the event would be held the
               following Tuesday from 5:30 to 7:30 in the Justice Parking Lot in Anderson, with a flyer
               to be posted on the marquee bulletin board to inform the public.
               Action: Motion by Commissioner  Chad  Mallett to Approve the proclamation
               proclaiming National Night Out in Grimes County, Texas, and  recognizing its
               importance in promoting  community  partnerships and  public safety. Seconded by
               Commissioner   Phillip Cox. Carried (5–0–0).

            6. Discuss and take action to properly classify Courthouse Security Deputies and
               make appropriate salary adjustments. —  Joe Fauth  III
               County Judge Joe Fauth opened the discussion by acknowledging that he had
               overlooked the two courthouse security deputies when considering salary classifications
               for other law enforcement personnel. Fauth explained that the courthouse deputies
               perform duties comparable to constable and sheriff's deputies, including responding to
               courtroom situations and conducting security screenings at the Justice Center entrance.
               He recommended   reclassifying them from level L5 to L6, which would require a budget
               amendment  of $17,833.27. Fauth proposed funding the adjustment by drawing from the
               sheriff's office machinery and equipment line, noting that the sheriff's department had
               approximately $515,000 in that account and that newer vehicles required fewer repairs.
               Commissioner Phillip Cox raised concern about whether the reduction might constrain
               the sheriff's department's ability to address equipment needs. Fauth indicated that any
               contingency funds could restore the amount later in the year if necessary. Cox also
               sought clarification on whether the sheriff's office had requested the reclassification, and
               Fauth confirmed that while the deputies had requested equal classification with other
               deputies, the final budgetary decision rested with him.
               Action: Motion by County Judge Joe Fauth  to Approve reclassification of
               courthouse security deputies from pay  grade L5 to L6, authorize a budget
               amendment   of $17,833.27 funded from the Sheriff's Office machinery and
               equipment  line, effective FY 2027 (October 1), and authorize the County Auditor to
               make the adjustment. Seconded   by Commissioner   Michael Raska. Carried (5–0–0).
            7. Discuss and take action on any necessary  amendments   to the FY2027 budget.  —

               County Auditor Jessi Murphy requested authorization to correct spelling errors in the

               Action: Motion by County Judge Joe Fauth  to Approve amendment    to the FY 2027

               corrections only, excluding changes to numerical figures. Seconded  by
               Commissioner   Chad Mallett. Carried (5–0–0).
