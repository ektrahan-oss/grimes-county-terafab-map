                                      SPECIAL   MEETING
                 OFFICIAL    MINUTES   —  THURSDAY,     NOVEMBER      12, 2026
The Grimes County Commissioners  Court met on Thursday, November  12, 2026, at 10:00 A.M.
Members  Present: County Judge Joe Fauth, Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos,
Commissioner Michael Raska, Commissioner  Phillip Cox.

County Judge Joe Fauth called the meeting to order.
Call to Order
Grimes County  Elections Administrator - Rachel Walker (JF) — Rachel  Walker, Grimes County
Elections Administrator
1. Consider  and take action to canvass the returns and declare the results of the November 3,
   2026 Gubernatorial  Election. — Rachel Walker, Grimes  County  Elections Administrator
       Elections Administrator Rachel Walker presented the official election returns for the November 3,
       2026 gubernatorial election. Her office completed tabulation of all early voting, election day, and
       eligible ballots processed by the early voting ballot board. The official results, compiled by
       precinct, were presented to the court along with supporting documentation for examination and
       canvassing. Walker requested that the court accept and declare the canvas.
   Motion  by County Judge  Joe Fauth  to Approve the canvass  of returns and declare the results
   of the November  3, 2026 Gubernatorial Election. Seconded  by Commissioner   Michael Raska.
   Carried (5–0–0).
Grimes County  Attorney - Megan  Barcak —  Megan  Barcak, Grimes  County  Attorney
2. Discuss  and take any necessary  action concerning pending  litigation, Cause No. D-1-GN-26-
   008509  SpaceX  v. Ken Paxton & Grimes  County  and Marie Egyed  v. Grimes County. —  Megan
   Barcak, Grimes  County  Attorney
       The court will go into closed session pursuant to Section 551.071 of the Texas Local
       Government  Code, for Consultations with Attorney regarding pending litigation.
       County Judge Joe Fauth opened  discussion of pending litigation in two matters: SpaceX v. Ken
       Paxton and Grimes County, and Marie Egyed v. Grimes County. The court entered closed
       session pursuant to Texas Local Government Code Section 551.071 to consult with its attorney
       regarding the pending litigation. Court convened at 10:04 AM. Upon returning from executive
       session at 11:27 AM, County Judge Joe Fauth reported that no decisions had been made during
       the closed discussion, although substantial discussion had occurred. County Judge Joe Fauth
       proposed authorizing County Attorney Megan Barcak to hire outside counsel to assist with the
       litigation and recommended an initial allocation of $125,000. County Auditor Jessi Murphy
       clarified that a budget line for litigation had been added that did not appear on the printed
       materials, and County Judge Joe Fauth revised the proposal to place the $125,000 allocation
       into Barcak's existing litigation budget line, bringing that account from $500 to $125,500. County
       Judge Joe Fauth instructed that Barcak keep the court informed as expenses were incurred and
       that she would oversee that budget line.
