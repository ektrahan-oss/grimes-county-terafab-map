Public Comments 
We look forward to hearing from the citizens of Grimes County. If you wish to make public comments you will 
need to submit a Public Participation Form prior to the start of the meeting and give it to the County Clerk. Public

18.  Consider and review the request from the Grimes County Republican Party regarding early voting 
     locations. 
     Grimes County Judge Joe Fauth III: There was a letter sent to Commissioners Court from the Grimes County 
     Republican Party Chairman, Alec Pointer. In summary what the letter states is that they understand the 
     financial consideration associated with expanding early voting locations in Plantersville and Iola, based on the 
     employee needs of the Elections Administrators office. It is their suggestion that they do not want to have 
     additional early voting locations set up for the November election, although the Court previously approved 
     adding 1 location north and 1 south. Commissioner Mallett felt that this was something that needed to be 
     discussed during budget. The Elections Administrator Rachel Walker was asked to put together a collective 
     number what the 2 additional locations would amount to based on what was submitted by the Iola 
     Community Center and Plantersville Town Hall for use of the facilities. Ms Walker estimated around 
     $125,000 for personnel, space rental and equipment. The other issue Ms Walker provided in conjunction with 
     the letter is that HB 2753 designates a change in the early election procedure, although will not take place 
     with the November election, but will need to be reviewed in 2027. Commissioner David Tullos stated he has 
     some questions for Mr Pointer and asked if this agenda item could be moved to the August 5th 
     Commissioners Court meeting to afford him the opportunity to answer in person the questions Mr Tullos has 
     about the letter. Judge Fauth stated Mr Pointer has a full time job so may not be able to attend the meeting, 
     but Mr Tullos could contact Mr Pointer personally. Commissioner Tullos states that he believes so of the 
     question should be a matter of public record. Mr Tullos continues that the letter is addressed to Judge Fauth 
     and it appears the Judge was present at the Republican County Executive Committee meeting. Judge Fauth 
     stated he was present for the meeting. Commissioner Tullos continues that at some point there was a vote by 
     the Grimes County Republican Executive Committee who voted not to support this particular expansion at 
     this time. It does not designate it's only for the November or primary it says they are not supporting expansion 
     at this time. since this impacts Plantersville and Todd Mission, which is in Mr Tullos precinct, it would be 
     important to know who was involved in the vote. Mr Tullos feels the constituents of Plantersville and Todd      Mission should know who in the Republican party chose not to support early voting for their area when there 
     have an overwhelming number of requests for early voting in these 2 areas. Mr Tullos states he has personally 
     witnessed long lines and people leaving the polling locations and not voting. He looks to see who is on the 
     Republican Party Executive Committee to see who was involved in this vote and who was there that 
     represents Plantersville and Todd Mission area. The precinct chair for Todd Mission is vacant so that area 
     was not represented. The other individual who was in a position to represent  that area is Sherry Fauth 
     precinct chair for Plantersville. It is Mr Tullos understanding that Ms Fauth voted against expanding early 
     voting in Plantersville for the constituents that she represents in that area. Mr Tullos will reach out to Mr 
     Pointer as he believes as a matter of public record the people of Plantersville and Todd Mission need to know 
     that the only precinct chair they elected to represent them on the Executive Committee voted against 
     expanding early voting to them.
