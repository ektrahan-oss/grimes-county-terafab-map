GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT
OFFICIAL MINUTES — THURSDAY, JULY 30, 2026

The Grimes County Commissioners Court met on Thursday, July 30, 2026, at 8:30 A.M. Members
Present: County Judge Joe Fauth, Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos,
Commissioner Michael Raska, Commissioner Phillip Cox.

1 Call to Order.
County Judge Joe Fauth III called the meeting to order

2 Consider and take action to approve the additional county match for the HAVA Election Security
Grant.

County Auditor Jessi Murphy explained that the court had previously approved a county match amount
for HAVA expenditures, as the grant does not reimburse one hundred percent of costs. An invoice
received by the auditor's office would exceed the previously approved match amount, necessitating
court action. Additionally, the state HAVA program requires both an invoice and proof of payment dated
on or before the grant expiration, rather than just an invoice with an expiration date, creating additional
urgency for the matter. There was much discussion between the Auditor's office, the Elections
Administrator and Commissioner Mallett concerning the dollar amount that the county needed to match

Action: A motion by County Judge Joe Fauth to approve the additional county match for the
HAVA Election Security Grant and authorize the Auditors office to make the necessary changes
and for the Treasurers office to prepare the check immediately. Seconded by Commissioner
Mallett. Motion carried (5-0-0)

3 Discuss and review proposed economic development 312 agreement with EVAPCO.

It is expected that the Commissioner Court will meet in a closed/executive session pursuant to Section
551.087 of the Texas Government Code to discuss commercial or financial information that the
Commissioners Court has received from EVAPCO, Inc. to propose a manufacturing facility for
heat-transfer equipment and thermal-management systems for cooling, refrigeration, and industrial
processing in the Navasota Industrial Park.

Action: No action was taken. The County Attorney has information to go back to Evapco and
speak with them.

ADJOURN.

A motion to adjourn the meeting was made by Commissioner Raska. Seconded by Commissioner
Cox. Carried. (5-0-0) The meeting adjourned at 8:51 AM.
