NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING ON THE PROPOSED DESIGNATION OF EVAPCO 
REINVESTMENT ZONE – 2026-001 BY THE COMMISSIONERS COURT OF GRIMES 
COUNTY, TEXAS 
 
On the 5th day of August, 2026 at 8:45 A.M. in the Grimes County Commissioner Courtroom, 
located in the Grimes County Justice & Business Center, 270 FM 149 W., Anderson, Texas in 
Grimes County, Texas, the Grimes County Commissioners Court will hold a public hearing on the 
matter of the proposed designation of certain property located near the Navasota Industrial Park 
and surrounding areas as a Reinvestment Zone under Subchapter C, Chapter 312 of the Texas 
Property Tax Code. This designation would allow for tax abatement agreements to be entered into 
by the County with owners of real property, personal property, equipment and fixtures within the 
Reinvestment Zone. The property to be considered for Reinvestment Zone designation is indicated 
in the Site Maps and Parcel Numbers shown below. 
 
The specific boundary of the proposed Reinvestment Zone is delineated in the Site Map Below, 
and it is intended for the construction, installation, and operation of a manufacturing facility of 
heat-transfer equipment and thermal management systems for cooling, refrigeration, and 
industrial process applications.  
 
At the hearing, interested persons are entitled to speak and present evidence for or against the 
designation.  Upon the close of the public hearing, the Commissioners Court intends to take action 
on whether or not to establish and designate the Reinvestment Zone.  Its designation would be 
“Evapco Reinvestment Zone No – 2026-001.”
