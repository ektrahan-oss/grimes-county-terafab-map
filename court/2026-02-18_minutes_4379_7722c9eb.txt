Public Comments 
First Public Speaker - Eloy Thomas: Resident of CR 206/207. Main concern is caliche can negatively impact lung 
health due to dust particles. Caliche can release fine dust when disturbed. Inhalation may lead to respiratory issues. 
Long term effects could lead to chronic respiratory disease. Mr Thomas wanted to make people aware of caliche on 
the roads especially for those that have lived there for multiple years. 
Second Public Speaker - Michael Chambers: Resident of CR 206. Paving 206/207 going south but leaving out 1/2 
mile on 206 going north. Mr Chambers is asking while the Road Dept has the equipment, materials on site to please 
pave the 1/2 mile going north. When 207 was paved it also included 207A and 207B. Asking if it could be 
considered to finish the remaining 1/2 mile. 
Third Public Speaker - Diana Boffa: Resident of CR 206 in Plantersville. Paving CR 206 S but 6/10 of a mile of CR 
206 N is being excluded. This particular stretch has the highest population. Ms Boffa stated they had been told the 
project could not be completed due to budget constraints and also that 206 N would not be paved due to it being a 
dead end road. Asking for priorities of roads to be reconsidered and finish the project on 206 N be paved as well. 
Fourth Public Speaker - Ron Boffa: Resident of CR 206. Lived on his property for 15 years. Brought in samples of 
Caliche for the Court to see. The large rocks are a concern for vehicle maintenance especially on tires. Other visuals 
presented were pictures with dollar bills on the road beside the rocks for reference to size. Points Mr Boffa would like to make is the concern of home values for some more expensive homes on this small part of 206 N. Safety 
concerns with the dust, lack of road conditions for children to walk or play on the road. Mr Boffa presented 
petitions from 8 other residents that could not be present in Court. Mr. Boffa stated he has not had a returned phone 
call from the County Engineer nor the County Commissioner.   
Commissioner Pct 2 David Tullos: A public comment submitted to Commissioner Tullos by a resident of CR 206 
who is a full time fire fighter in a neighboring county and could not be present for Court as he is on duty today. His 
comment is to express his concern for the current paving plan on CR 206 that the north half will not be paved. The 
north half represents 11 property owners. He is asking for the paving project to include this small portion of the 
road so it will be completed. Paving the road creates long term benefits such as improved safety, reliable emergency 
access, reduced maintenance and long term costs, dust reduction for health benefits, protection of vehicles and 
property values. Commissioner Tullos thanked the residents for being present in Court and that he does return 
phone calls.  He spoke with several residents this week.

10.  Receive the revised Grimes County Constable Pct 3/4 Racial Profiling report for 2025. 
     Constable Pct 3/4 Gary Bender: When submitting Racial Profiling Report for 2025 there was a glitch 
     discovered in the crime system when data was retrieved from traffic stops. It was brought to IT dept attention. 
     The software analyst contacted the Constable on the format they were using to retrieve the data on the traffic 
     stops. The problem was basically when the deputy would be out on a traffic stop, if there was a secondary or 
     third unit, when he put in his unit to retrieve the data it added those additional units on traffic stops to his unit 
     therefore inflating the numbers of his traffic stops.  That caused the discrepancy. To make sure the Racial 
     Profiling data was correct, a printout of all traffic stops for the year, a spreadsheet was created to make sure 
     they lined up with the accurate number. Hard copies have to be kept until data can be retrieved electronically. 
     Going forward there will be a hard copy for verbal warnings and a hard copy for the actual citation. This 
     gives us an accurate number regardless what the electronic data reads.

14.  Discuss and take action on the $18,161 cost to remove illegally dumped tires from Holland Creek within      County Road 403 right of way. 
     County Engineer Jon Steiber: A cost estimate has been put together to remove illegally dumped tires from 
     Holland Creek within CR 403 right-of-way. Personnel, equipment and rental equipment, 3 days of work with 
     one crew and the Diversion crew. Total is approximately $18,161.00. Technically the property that the 
     County maintains, this falls in our right-of-way. The equipment contracts are funds needed from somewhere 
     identified by the Court for rental equipment such as a crane to lift each tire out. A semi would have to be on 
     site after tires are removed. Crew will be working on this for approx 1 week which could be spent prepping 
     roads. No quotes have been obtained from contractors at this time, but would probably add an additional 
     $10,000 to the cost. Judge Joe Fauth stated that he has had a conversation with Mike Harris and the pipeline 
     coming through can absorb the use of several hundred used tires. Reach out to the pipeline that they may be 
     willing to participate with the clean up to help alleviate some of the cost to the County. Commissioner Tullos 
     asked if a County policy needs to be put in place as to how the County will address illegal dumping? Other 
     areas of the County are experiencing the same problems. One in particular was on CR 202 where 
     individual(s) are dumping their household trash. County Judge suggests we can hopefully identify from 
     household trash who it belongs to. Commissioner Tullos asked who do we have investigating? Road & 
     Bridge crews or the Sheriffs Dept? Commissioner Cox stated that he realizes there is not a policy but these 
     tires needs to be cleaned up immediately.  
     Motion by Phillip Cox to Approve to spend $18,161.00 cost to remove illegally dumped tires from Holland 
     Creek within CR 403 right-of-way to be deducted from the Contingency line item in the budget. Seconded by 
     David Tullos. 
     Commissioner Cox commented that tires are not biodegradable. and there are multiple areas in the County 
     that experience illegal dumping. In regards to policy he believes it is time for the Court to discuss what 
     aggressive actions to pursue in regards to illegal dumping which will give the Court the authority to take 
     action. Commissioner Tullos concurred that this be placed on a future agenda to discuss a policy. Judge Fauth 
     reminded Grimes County citizens if you witness illegal dumping please report it. There are much better ways 
     to spend tax dollars. Especially when there are residents asking to have additional roads paved and the County 
     has to spend $18,161.00 to clean up illegal dumping.
