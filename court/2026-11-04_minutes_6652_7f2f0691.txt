GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT

PUBLIC HEARING
OFFICIAL MINUTES — WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 4, 2026
The Grimes County Commissioners Court met on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 8:45 A.M.
Members Present: County Judge Joe Fauth, Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos,
Commissioner Michael Raska, Commissioner Phillip Cox.

County Judge Joe Fauth called the meeting to order.
Call to Order

Grimes County Director of Development Services - Shawn King (CM) — Shawn King, Grimes
County Director of Development Services

1. Consider the application for and hear any protest to the proposed replat of Country Town
Estates Lot 105 located in Precinct 1. — Shawn King, Grimes County Director of Development
Services

County Judge Joe Fauth introduced the application for replat of Country Town Estates Lot 105 in
Precinct 1. Director of Development Services Shawn King reported that the property owner was
splitting the lot into two parcels, each approximately one and a half acres, with public water and
all utilities available. King confirmed that the proposed split met subdivision regulations. County
Judge Joe Fauth asked Commissioner Mallett, in whose precinct the property is located, whether
he had any objection to the replat. The County Judge opened the matter as a public hearing and
invited any members of the public to speak regarding the replat. No one came forward to
address the matter. County Judge Joe Fauth made a final call for any comments from
commissioners or the public and, receiving none, proceeded to close discussion.

Motion by Commissioner Chad Mallett to adjourn the public hearing. Seconded by Commissioner
Michael Raska. Carried (5-0-0). The meeting adjourned at 8:49 AM.

THE OFFICIAL MINUTES OF THE GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT ON November 4th,
2026, ARE HEREBY APPROVED.

County Clerk, Barbara Kimich
