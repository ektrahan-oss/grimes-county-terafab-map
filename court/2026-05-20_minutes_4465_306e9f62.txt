13.  Discuss and take action to replace Constable Pct 1 mobile radio. 
     Grimes County Constable Precinct 1 James Ellis: Background for his request: In 2023, at that time, Constable 
     Schaper was involved in an on-duty auto accident. Another patrol unit was purchased, which Constable Ellis 
     is currently driving. The equipment that was in the old patrol unit is in Constable Ellis' patrol car. In 
     December 2025, Emergency Management Coordinator Dione Bumpous emailed Constable Ellis concerning 
     BVWAC switching to basically encrypting all law enforcement radios within the Brazos Valley. Constable 
     Ellis brought his patrol unit to Ms. Bumpous, where she attempted to encrypt his radio but realized she did 
     not have the necessary equipment to do so. After research discovered this particular radio was manufactured 
     in 1991, therefore were not able to upgrade and encrypt those channels. Constable Ellis tried to outsource as 
     much as he could, without coming before Court. He asked Chief Deputy Martha Smith if the Sheriffs Dept 
     had any radios, which they did not. He reached out to Sargeant Dominguez with DPS, they could donate, 
     which they did not. Ms Crenshaw and Ms Bumpous went though the process for a grant from BVCOG which 
     is still pending. The reason this is now being expedited is that the Sheriffs Office and Dispatch have officially 
     switched over to the fully encrypted channels. This leaves Constable Ellis without comes in his patrol unit. He 
     does have a hand-held radio from last year, but cannot continue to run patrol with only the hand-held radio. 
     Total needed $6,695.44. Commissioner Chad Mallet asked County Auditor Jessi Murphy for the balance in 
     the 424 Contigency fund. She stated there is approximately $100,000 but have yet to do the amendment for 
     Road Dept from the clean up. Waiting on the invoices. This should leave approximately $90,000. 
     Motion by Chad Mallett to Approve to move funds from 424 Contingency to Constable James Ellis budget to

18.  Discuss and take action to approve or deny a waiver valuation policy in support of property acquisitions for 
     the CR 409/Pinebrook GLO MIT-MOD grant. 
     Grimes County Engineer Jon Steiber: Kevin Pell from Grantworks, who is our grant administrator for this 
     grant, to speak on how to get this County project moving again. Waiver Valuation Policy that is needed from 
     the previous April 1st Court agenda, with the help of the County Attorney, Mr. Steiber has revised some of 
     the language. Mr Pell wanted to appear before Court in case there were questions or concerns regarding the 
     policy. It is specific to general land office grants that waiver valuations are required for and specific to that 
     purpose. It is not broadly for any acquisition that the County does or any acquisitions on GLO projects that 
     would require full appraisal. It is specific to this process when the evaluation falls under $15,000. From the 
     GLO's implementation manual, it lays out the process that the County follows to conduct these waiver 
     valuation forms and gives the County Judge the authorization to sign off on the waiver valuation forms. This 
     is the next step required in the acquisition process. Once these forms are signed, they will move forward with 
     the offer packets to be sent out, in accordance with the HUD regulations, to the property owners. Precinct 2 
     Commissioner David Tullos wanted to thank Mr Pell, Mr Steiber and the County Attorney, Megan Barcak, 
     for taking the time to finesse the document to make it more acceptable to Commissioners Court. Mr Tullos 
     read directly from the document what he believes is the key phrase. Grimes County Commissioners Court 
     reserves the right under local Government Code 261 to not pursue involuntary acquisition and to cease 
     negotiations at any time during the appraisal or acquisition process. Mr Tullos states the reason that it is of 
     value is that in local Government Code Section 261 it specifically assigns the authority to the Commissioners 
     Court, wherein the Statute reads: The condemnation proceeding must be instituted in the name of the county 
     and under the direction of Commissioners Court. 
     Motion by David Tullos to Approve a waiver valuation policy in support of property acquisitions for the CR 
     409/Pinebrook GLO MIT-MOD grant. Seconded by Phillip Cox.

22.  Discuss and take action regarding the Credit Card Policy as it relates to the Historical Commission and Child 
     Welfare Board. 
     Grimes County Auditor Jessi Murphy: Current policy states that only County employees can be issued credit 
     cards, but given that both the Historical Commission and Child Welfare Boards operate under the direction of 
     the Court, Ms Murphy is asking for guidance from Court for the use of county credit card for board-related 
     expenses. Judge Fauth asked if there is a credit limit imposed on the credit cards. Ms Murphy stated the 
     answer would come from the Treasurers Office as she is not sure how the cards are set up. The Judge states 
     he brings the matter up because they are not county employees, although they represent the county. Ms 
     Murphy offered 2 solutions: The Court could make an exception or create/change a policy on board members 
     and require that an employee or a Commissioner be a board member. Commissioner Cox stated he is the 
     liaison to the Child Welfare Board. The current resident of the board was a county employee but is no longer 
     employed by the county. Judge Fauth asked Commissioner Tullos, the liaison to the Historical Commission, 
     his thoughts on issuing credit cards. Mr Tullos stated that Mary Ann Waters, who is a county employee, is on 
     the board. Mr Tullos also stated that he is not opposed to amending the appointment policy to include a 
     county employee or Commissioners Court member on any of the boards that are representations of the 
     county. Ms Murphy received confirmation from the Treasurer's office that limits can be put on the credit 
     cards.  
     Motion by Phillip Cox to Approve to make an exception to the credit card policy for the Historical 
     Commission and Child Welfare Board in the event the board member assigned a credit card is not an 
     employee that they will still be allowed to retain and be subject to all the policies of the county.  Seconded by 
     David Tullos.

     obligation to not be a bad actor and the county would have to pursue civil charges. 
     Amended Motion by Phillip Cox to Approve to make an exception to the credit card policy for the Historical

     employee that they will still be allowed to retain and be subject to all the policies of the county. Also, to
