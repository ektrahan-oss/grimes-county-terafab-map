GRIMES COUNTY BUDGET HEARINGS
OFFICIAL MINUTES — WEDNESDAY, JULY 29, 2026

The Grimes County Budget Hearings met on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 9:00 A.M. Members Present:
County Judge Joe Fauth, Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos, Commissioner Michael
Raska, Commissioner Phillip Cox.

County Judge Joe Fauth called the meeting to order.

Discuss and review the Grimes County FY 2027 departmental budget requests. — Joe Fauth Ill

County Judge Joe Fauth opened the morning's budget review sessions by welcoming attendees and
establishing the schedule for the day, which included presentations from the tax appraisal office,
juvenile probation, nonprofits, and maintenance departments. The Chief Appraiser Mark Boehnke
presented the tax appraisal budget of $793,231, noting that the appraised value of Grimes County had
increased from approximately $5.5 billion to $5.7 billion. Boehnke explained that the final budget
amount could fluctuate based on the tax rates set by schools, the county, and cities, as the appraisal
district's bill is calculated as a percentage of the total tax levy. He reported that collections for 2025
taxes were tracking well and discussed the ongoing annual aerial flyover program, which he credited
as a significant cost-saving measure that had evolved from once every three years to annually under
the court's approval. Boehnke noted the potential impact of recent legislative changes, including
increased homestead exemptions for seniors and expanded property tax caps, which had removed
substantial value from the tax roll. Commissioner Phillip Cox inquired about whether the flyover used
an airplane or drone and received clarification that it was an actual aircraft that conducted the survey in
late December through early January, with processed results delivered around March on data disks
that were loaded onto county servers. The discussion turned to the appraised value increases and
potential new development, particularly regarding SpaceX and TerraFab projects, though Boehnke
expressed cautious skepticism, stating he would not believe in major development until he saw
physical evidence of construction activity. Following the tax appraisal office discussion, the court briefly
addressed the animal rescue facility after representatives Michele Gremillion and others presented the
Grimes County Animal Rescue budget. The court took a tour of the facility to visually assess its
condition. Upon returning, Commissioner David Tullos delivered an extensive commentary noting the
facility's deteriorating walls, persistent odor issues, and the significant increase in law enforcement
animal engagements from one in 2024 to 201 in 2025, which had generated approximately $66,000 in
expenses. Gremillion explained that the rescue had requested $50,000 in funding but was initially
budgeted at $12,000 in the preliminary document. Judge Fauth proposed allocating $20,000 to the
animal rescue for operational costs and tasked Facilities Director Rodney Floyd with determining what
additional funds might be needed from the facilities budget for building maintenance, as the structure,
though operated by the private nonprofit, was county-owned property under a ten-year lease
agreement. County Auditor Jessi Murphy and others reviewed numerous budget line items across
multiple departments and funds throughout the day, including discussions of IT requests for contract
management and a Planet Bids system, professional services, equipment purchases, and capital
improvement priorities. The court engaged in substantial discussion regarding the anticipated SpaceX
