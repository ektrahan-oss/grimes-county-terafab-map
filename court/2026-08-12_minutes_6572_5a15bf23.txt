                 GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT
                  OFFICIAL MINUTES — WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 12, 2026

The Grimes County Commissioners Court met on Wednesday, August 12, 2026, at 9:00 A.M. Members
Present: County Judge Joe Fauth, Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos,
Commissioner Michael Raska, Commissioner Phillip Cox.

County Judge Joe Fauth called the meeting to order.

 — Regular
Call to Order
Public Comments
  We look forward to hearing from the citizens of Grimes County. If you wish to make public comments
  you will need to submit a Public Participation Form prior to the start of the meeting and give it to the
  County Clerk. Public comments will be limited to 2 minutes per person.
  County Judge Joe Fauth made a public comment addressing Facebook concerns about burning
  operations for the TeraFab project. Judge Fauth reported that TeraFab and its contractor maintain a
  twenty-four hour daily watch, monitor weather and wind reports, and refrain from burning during
  unfavorable wind conditions. He noted that the contractor keeps burn piles small to minimize ash
  spread, maintains multiple water trucks on scene for dust control and fire suppression, and operates
  within well-defined containment areas. Judge Fauth stated that dispatch, emergency management
  services coordinator Dion Bumpus, and the Iola fire department have been notified of the burns in their
  response area. He acknowledged a recent smoke complaint occurred during cloud cover conditions
  that naturally held smoke down, and he confirmed that the contractor has been maintaining at least a
  five-hundred foot clear line from the burn piles. Judge Fauth shared this information with both the
  commissioners and the public to address concerns and prepare commissioners for potential citizen
  calls on the matter.

Grimes County Sheriff's Office - Chief Deputy Martha Smith (JF)
    2  Discuss and take action to approve 2 new Jail Sergeant positions for the Grimes County
      Jail. — Martha Smith, Grimes County Sheriff's Office
       County Judge Joe Fauth presented a request from the Grimes County Sheriff's Office to approve
       two new jail sergeant positions for the Grimes County Jail. Human Resource Director Atina Adair
       indicated that the office was discussing salary ranges in the three to four range and noted that
       candidates had not yet been identified, meaning positions could potentially be filled from outside or
       through internal promotion. County Judge Joe Fauth clarified that the office needed to determine
       whether the positions would be filled by promoting current jailers or hiring from outside, as this

       statute. County Auditor Jessi Murphy explained that adding positions to the current fiscal year

       resources, but if outside hires were contemplated, the funding situation was less clear. The matter
       was discussed at length, but the court ultimately decided to take no action at this time and instead
       direct the Sheriff's Office to proceed with filling the existing vacant sergeant position through the
       normal hiring process, with further consideration of the two additional positions to occur during the
       fiscal year 2027 budget discussion.
Grimes County Judge - Joe Fauth III
    3  Discuss and take action on any issue regarding FY 27 Proposed Budget. — Joe Fauth III,
      Grimes County Judge
       County Auditor Jessi Murphy presented detailed changes to the FY 2027 budget following
       discussions with various departments. She reported reallocating IT expenses for the fire marshal
       to fund 63, reducing the Fire Marshal Fire Inspector software line by $7,002.41 and the IT
       computer hardware line by $2,965 to cover a computer and monitor, with the cell phone line
       reduced by $675. All these amounts were added to fund 63 with the same line items created there.
       Murphy met with representatives regarding direct victim expenses and reallocated the existing
       $25,000 across ten different line items, including lodging for $8,000, dining for $5,000, food and
       drink stock for $2,500, fuel for $2,000, transportation for $2,500, repairs and maintenance for
       vehicles for $1,200, clothing and grooming for $1,500, beneficiary card assistance for $1,100,
       security locks for $500, and a communication line for $700, maintaining the total at $25,000.
       County Judge Joe Fauth and the court discussed at length the budget grand totals under different
       scenarios, including one removing the SpaceX revenue of $10,000,000, which resulted in a
       $12,869,414.55 deficit, and another adjusted scenario including removal of the fairgrounds capital
       project, reduction of nondepartmental contingency by $1,800,000, reallocation of IT expenses, and
       addition of two replacement ambulances, which with the $10,000,000 in revenue would leave a
       $1,700,000 excess. Murphy walked the court through multiple salary increase scenarios ranging
       from a $4 increase across the board to fixed rate and percentage options, with the court ultimately
       moving toward discussion of a dollar 15 COLA with merit-based increases and adjustments to
       bring part-time employees to minimum wage levels. The discussion was substantive and
       wide-ranging, touching on capital project funding, ambulance purchases, constable staffing, tax
       rates, and numerous budget line items across multiple departments.
