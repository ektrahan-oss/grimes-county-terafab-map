                     The Minutes of the Regular Meeting are as follows: 
 
Call to Order 

Grimes County Engineer - Jon Steiber 

1.   Consider public comment on adopting an Order Establishing a School Zone and Reducing the Speed Limit on 
     County Roads in Richards, Texas. 
     Grimes County Judge Joe Fauth III called the meeting to order @ 8:45 am 
     County Engineeer - Jon Steiber: Pct 2 Commissioner David Tullos linked up the Road Department with 
     Richards ISD organization to set up a school zone and reduce speed limits around the school during school 
     hours, which will begin @ 7:00 am to 4:00 pm Monday through Friday, and include the following sections: 
     Mulberry Street, Colorado Street, Panther Drive, Trinity Street and Guadalupe Street. The process required by 
     Texas Transportation Code 251.152 was followed, post the notice of a Public Hearing. School Zone 
     boundaries were set. Signage is all paid for by Richards ISD, Road Dept will install the signs within the right 
     of way. Everything follows the manual for uniform traffic control devices. This allows the speed limits to be 
     enforceable with no defense if speed limit is not observed. The timeline for the signs to be installed, is a 
     couple of weeks.  

Adjourn 
Motion by David Tullos to Adjourn. Seconded by Chad Mallett. 
 
In Favor: 5 
Opposed: 0 
Motion Passed 
 
Meeting was adjourned

ORDER ESTABLISHING A SCHOOL ZONE AND REDUCING SPEED LIMIT DURING 
         SCHOOL HOURS ON COUNTY ROADS IN RICHARDS, TEXAS              
                          GRIMES COUNTY, TEXAS 
 
STATE OF TEXAS          § 
                        §           KNOWN ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS: 
COUNTY OF GRIMES        §  
 
     WHEREAS,    on January 21 st, 2026  the  Grimes County Commissioners Court held 
a public  hearing  and  subsequently  met  in  a  regular  session  to  consider  establishing  a 
school zone and reducing  the speed limit to  20 miles per hour during school hours of 7:00
AM to 4:00 PM Monday through Friday on sections of Mulberry Street, Colorado Street,
Panther Drive, Trinity Street, and  Guadalupe Street for  the public safety of residents in
Precinct 2, Grimes County, Texas and  for the students of Richards Independent School
District; and 
      
     WHEREAS,    notice  of the public hearing was properly published in the Navasota  
Examiner as required by TEXAS TRANSPORTATION CODE     §251.152;  and 
      
     WHEREAS,    a  motion  was made  and  seconded  to approve  this  Order  Reducing 
Speed Limit  on on  sections  of  Mulberry  Street,  Colorado  Street,  Panther  Drive,  Trinity 
Street, and  Guadalupe Street, Grimes County, Texas to 20 miles  per hour; and 
 
     NOW,   THEREFORE,    IT IS ORDERED  by the County of Grimes, State of Texas 
that the  speed limit on Mulberry  Street, Colorado Street, Panther Drive, Trinity Street, and 
Guadalupe Street  shall  be  20  miles  per  hour  during  the  hours  of  7:00  AM  to  4:00  PM 
Monday  through  Friday  when  school  is  in  session.  This  Order shall  be  immediately 
effective upon approval by the  Grimes County Commissioners  Court. 
 
     Issued  by Authority of the Commissioners Court of Grimes County, Texas this 21 st  
day of January,  2026 .
 
PASSED and ADOPTED on this   21 st day  of January,  2026.
