GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT

OFFICIAL MINUTES — WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2026
The Grimes County Commissioners Court met on Wednesday, October 7, 2026, at 8:45 A.M. Members
Present: Commissioner Chad Mallett, Commissioner David Tullos, Commissioner Michael Raska,
Commissioner Phillip Cox. County Judge Joe Fauth was not present for the meeting. Commissioner
Chad Mallett called the meeting to order.

Call to Order

Grimes County Director of Development Services - Shawn King (DT/PC) — Shawn King,
Development Services

1. Consider the application for and hear any protest to the proposed replat for The
Cedars Section 1 block 3 lot 10 and 11 located in Precinct 2. — Shawn King,
Development Services

Commissioner Chad Mallett introduced the proposed replat for The Cedars Section 1
Block 3, Lot 10 and 11 located in Precinct 2.

2. Consider the application for and hear any protest to the proposed replat for Foster
Estates section 1 block 3 Lot 9 located in Precinct 4. — Shawn King, Development
Services

Director of Development Services Shawn King reported on a replat application for Foster
Estates Section 1 Block 3, Lot 9 in Precinct 4 that had been filed several years earlier
but never brought to the Commissioners Court. All paperwork and fees had been
completed at that time under the old regulations. When the applicant inquired about the
status four to five months prior, the application was found to meet the requirements
under the grandfathered regulations in effect when it was filed. Commissioner Chad
Mallett asked whether the county engineer had reviewed the application during the
regulatory transition period.

A motion to adjourn the meeting was made Commissioner Michael Raska. Seconded by Commissioner
Chad Mallett. Carried. The meeting adjourned at 8:50 AM.

THE OFFICIAL MINUTES OF THE GRIMES COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COURT ON October 7th,
2026, ARE HEREBY APPROVED.

Barbara Kimich County Clerk
