 Public Hearing: 
 A public hearing will be held on the possible designation of land located near the Gibbons Creek Reservoir and surrounding areas as a 
 reinvestment zone pursuant to Chapter 312 of the Texas Tax Code. The zone would be identified as “SpaceX Reinvestment Zone No. 
 01-2026-001”. 

 1.   Discussion and action on the designation and creation of SpaceX Reinvestment Zone No. 01-2026-001 pursuant to Chapter 312 
      of the Texas Tax Code. 

 Public Hearing: 
 A public hearing will be held on a proposed Tax Abatement Agreement between Space Exploration Technologies Corp. and Grimes 
 County, Texas pursuant to Chapter 312 of the Texas Tax Code. 

 2.   Discussion and action on approving a Tax Abatement Agreement between Space Exploration Technologies Corp. and Grimes 
      County, Texas pursuant to Chapter 312 of the Texas Tax Code.  It is expected that the Commissioners Court will meet in a 
      closed/executive session pursuant to Section 551.087 of the Texas Government Code to discuss or deliberate regarding 
      commercial or financial information. 

 3.   Discussion and action on approving a Section 381.004 Economic Development Program and Agreement between Space 
      Exploration Technologies Corp. and Grimes County, Texas. It is expected that the Commissioners Court will meet in a 
      closed/executive session pursuant to Section 551.087 of the Texas Government Code to discuss or deliberate regarding 
      commercial or financial information.
