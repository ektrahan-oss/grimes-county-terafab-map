Call to Order 
Meeting is called to order by County Judge Joe Fauth III @ 8:45 am 

Grimes County Attorney - Megan Barcak (JF) 

1.   Discuss and hear public comment on amendments to the Executive Summary of Guidelines and Criteria for 
     Tax Abatements Pursuant to Texas Tax Code Chapter 312 and for Grants Pursuant to Texas Local 
     Government Code Chapter 381. 
     Grimes County Attorney Megan Barcak: Today's meeting is to talk about guidelines for tax abatement and 
     381 that is currently in place under the tax code of 312. The guidelines can be amended by three-fourths (3/4) 
     vote of the Court after a Public Hearing has been held on te subject. Currently the proposed amendments are 
     on page 8 & 10, the removal of the electricity language from ineligible property definition and on page 13 & 
     14 a revise of the committee to allow for community representation. Currently the proposal is that the 
     committee will be made up of the County Judge, the Commissioner whose precinct the proposed project will 
     be in, a representative of the Grimes County business community, 4 representative from Grimes County 
     community one from each precinct, a representative from the CAD department and a Grimes County 
     employee representative.  
     Public Speakers: Karen Shiver Grimes County Democrats, Donna o'Connor, Shiara Trumble Grimes County 
     Citizens for Responsible Development, Glyna McWhorter Brown, Michael Hart, Pam Wehmeyer, Donna H. 
     Canney,  Les Dillenbeck, Jerry Gilliland, Glenda Deweese Grimes County Citizens for Responsible 
     Development, Debra Morwood Grimes County Committee for Responsible Growth, Sandra Woodruf, Jerry 
     Ellis, Debra Sanders, Marie Egyed Grimes County Citizens for Responsible Development, Mackie Bobo, 
     Bobby Jo Swafford Grimes County Citizens for Responsible Development, Ricky Weinkauf. 
     Concerns of making changes to the tax abatements.
