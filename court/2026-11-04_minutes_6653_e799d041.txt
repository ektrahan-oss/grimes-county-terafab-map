County Engineer Jon Steiber about the contract timeline. Tullos noted that the contract showed
an October 9 start date despite the meeting occurring in November and asked whether the dates
would be adjusted. County Engineer Jon Steiber confirmed that a start date would be established
after contract approval and indicated the contractor believed the work could be completed this
year, potentially faster than the ninety-day estimate.

Commissioner Tullos then raised concerns about property owners along the roads who had
extended fences into the county right-of-way, particularly in areas where culvert placement and
replacement would occur. He inquired how the county would notify residents that fences might
need to be removed to restore the original ditches and asked whether the work would restore the
original ditch alignment or create irregular patterns to work around existing encroachments.
County Engineer Steiber responded that the road alignment did not impact those encroachments
and that the county would do as much work as possible without extensive additional work. The
discussion continued at length regarding how specific situations with fence encroachments would
be handled, with Steiber suggesting Tullos email his concerns so he could submit the question to
the project engineer. Tullos indicated he preferred to discuss the matter directly with Steiber to
understand the county's notification and fence removal approach.

Discussion and possible action regarding a request from Grimes County Youth Football for
use of the Main Hall at the Grimes County Fairgrounds for their end-of-year banquet,
including consideration of rental fees and previously established rental agreement deadlines.
— Rodney Floyd, Grimes County Facilities

County Judge Joe Fauth introduced a request from Grimes County Youth Football for use of the
main hall at Grimes County Fairgrounds for their end-of-year banquet, noting that the
organization sought consideration of rental fees and previously established rental agreement
deadlines. Facilities Director Rodney Floyd explained that the county had been working with the
organization and that the court would need to determine whether to grant any discounted rate or
waive the sixty-day scheduling requirement. Board Member Macy Savant and the organization's
president, Tracy Rush, presented their request. Savant outlined that Grimes County Youth
Football Association serves over 175 children annually, with 75 placed on payment plans and 30
requiring sponsorship assistance to participate. The organization also hosted a free summer
camp that welcomed over 200 children with assistance from 63 volunteers. Savant explained that
sponsorships received are directed back into youth programs and that the organization requests
the county fully waive rental fees as an in-kind sponsorship to support their mission. She noted
that the banquet, scheduled for December 13, 2026, would accommodate approximately 300 to
400 attendees and would celebrate children, parents, coaches, and volunteers while recognizing
those who make the organization possible.

County Judge Joe Fauth clarified whether any field competitions would occur inside the facility,
and Board Member Savant confirmed none would. Commissioner Phillip Cox asked whether the
organization would still provide required security under the contract, which Savant confirmed.
County Attorney Megan Barcak requested clarification that the court was waiving fees rather
than providing a sponsorship and recommended waiving the thirty-day timeline requirement
given the event's scheduled date and the need for the organization to complete contractual
obligations.

Motion by Commissioner Phillip Cox to Approve the request from Grimes County Youth
Football to waive the rental fees for use of the Main Hall at the Grimes County Fairgrounds for
their end-of-year banquet on December 13, 2026, waiving the sixty-day scheduling deadline,
with all other contract requirements including security remaining in place, and noting it is a
fee waiver not a sponsorship. Seconded by Commissioner Michael Raska. Carried (5-0-0).

26. Consider and take action to amend employee policy section "2A-7 Pay Periods". — Atina
Adair, Grimes County Human Resources

Human Resource Director Atina Adair presented an amendment to employee policy section 2A-7
regarding pay periods. Adair explained that the last pay period of the fiscal year and the first of
the new fiscal year require complex processing by the treasurer's and auditor's offices. During
these periods, many employees called reporting late paychecks, though the paychecks were

actually on time. The confusion arose because the treasurer's office typically closes payroll by
Wednesday, causing checks to arrive early, leading some employees to believe payday was
Wednesday or Thursday rather than Friday. Adair requested adding the word "Friday" to the
policy language to clarify that payday falls on Fridays according to dates established by the
Commissioners Court. County Treasurer Tom Maynard added that banks themselves sometimes
contribute to confusion by giving customers access to deposits a day early, so some employees
see funds in their accounts on Thursday or even Wednesday. Adair reiterated that the written
clarification would help prevent misunderstandings among county employees.

Motion by County Judge Joe Fauth to Amend employee policy section 2A-7 Pay Periods to
add that payday will fall on Fridays according to the dates established by the Commissioners
Court. Seconded by Commissioner Michael Raska. Carried (5-0-0).
