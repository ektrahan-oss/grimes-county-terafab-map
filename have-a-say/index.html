How a TCEQ permit moves

  1. First noticeYou can comment as soon as it's published.
  2. Draft permit noticeUsually at least 30 days to comment or request a meeting or hearing.
  3. TCEQ responds to commentsA final 30-day window to request a contested case hearing.
  4. DecisionBy TCEQ staff, or by the commissioners if a hearing is asked for.

Water rights usually have a single notice with a 30-day clock, and some are shorter. Always go by the dates on the actual notice.
