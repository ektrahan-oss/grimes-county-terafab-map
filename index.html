About me and this map

I'm a market intelligence and GIS professional based in the Houston area. My family owns land in Grimes County, which is part of why I started following this project closely. I built this map to understand what Terafab means for the county, and I'm sharing it for free so others can too. Nothing here is legal, financial or investment advice.
